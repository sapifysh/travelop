GILI ISLANDS PRIVATE TOUR

The Gili Islands by private boat, without the crowded ferries.

Gili Trawangan, Gili Meno and Gili Air are a short boat ride from Lombok's north coast. On a FIRST-LOP private tour, your driver takes you to the harbour and your own boat and captain take it from there: turtle reefs, the underwater statues and quiet beaches, on your schedule.

From $110 / private charter

How the day works

Your private driver picks you up anywhere on mainland Lombok and drives you to Teluk Nare harbour. There, a private wooden outrigger boat and a local captain are reserved only for your group. You snorkel with wild green sea turtles, visit the underwater statues off Gili Meno, and stop on Gili Trawangan for lunch and a car-free bicycle ride before the boat returns you to your driver.

Why a private Gili tour

Public boats run on fixed timetables and fill up in high season. A private charter leaves when you are ready, goes where you want, and lets you stay longer at the best snorkel spots. It also suits families and couples who want a quiet day on the water.

Good to know before you go

The day takes about 7 to 9 hours including the drive. Bring reef-safe sunscreen, a hat and cash for lunch on the islands. Sea conditions are calmest in the morning, so we usually start early.

FAQ

How much is a private Gili Islands tour from Lombok?

FIRST-LOP private Gili Islands tours start from $110 per private charter. Your exact quote depends on your group size and pickup location.

Which harbour do we leave from?

Private tours usually depart from Teluk Nare harbour in North Lombok. Your driver takes you there from your hotel or villa.

Can we see sea turtles?

Yes. Wild green sea turtles live on the reefs around the Gili Islands, and your captain takes you to the spots where they are most often seen.

Is snorkel gear included?

Tell us when you book and we will arrange snorkel gear for your group.
