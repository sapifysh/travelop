SOUTH LOMBOK TOUR

South Lombok's best beaches in one private day.

The south coast is why most people fall for Lombok: wide white bays, green headlands and surf that rolls in all year. A private South Lombok tour links the best of it in one relaxed day, ending with sunset on the hill at Bukit Merese.

From $65 / vehicle (up to 4 guests)

A typical South Lombok itinerary

Morning swim at Selong Belanak, the gentlest beach on the coast and a good place for first-time surfers. Lunch in Kuta Mandalika. Afternoon at Tanjung Aan's powdery sand and hidden coves. Finish on Bukit Merese for golden hour over the twin bays. The full day takes about 8 to 10 hours, and every stop can be changed.

Add the Sasak village of Sade

On the way south, stop at Sade, a traditional Sasak hamlet with thatched houses and handwoven textiles. It adds about an hour and gives the beach day some local culture.

Who this tour is for

Beach lovers, photographers, families and surf beginners. If you are staying in Senggigi, Mataram or near the airport, this is the easiest way to see the south without renting a scooter.
