How hiring a driver in Lombok works

Tell us your dates, where you are staying and roughly what you want to see. We match you with a car sized for your group and a chauffeur who knows the route. On the day, your driver picks you up at your hotel, villa or Lombok International Airport (LOP) and stays with you for the whole day. Change plans as you go: stay longer at a beach, skip a stop, or ask for the warung locals actually eat at.

Cars for every group size

FAQ

How much does a private driver in Lombok cost?

With FIRST-LOP, a car with a private driver starts from $40 a day for a compact car. Seven-seat cars such as the Xpander start from $60 a day, premium Innova Zenix models from $85, and the Toyota Alphard is $200 a day.

Is fuel included?

Yes. Fuel for your planned itinerary, road tolls and destination parking fees are included in your daily price.

Can the driver pick us up from Lombok Airport?

Yes. Your driver can meet you at the arrivals hall of Lombok International Airport (LOP), help with luggage and start your day straight from the airport.

How far ahead should I book?

We recommend 24 to 48 hours ahead, and earlier in high season (July to September and the year-end holidays). Same-day requests are often possible depending on availability.
