Where we drive from the airport

Lombok International Airport sits in Central Lombok, close to the south coast. The most requested transfers are to Kuta Mandalika and Tanjung Aan, Selong Belanak and the southwest surf beaches, Senggigi on the west coast, and Teluk Nare or Bangsal harbour for boats to Gili Trawangan, Gili Meno and Gili Air.

FAQ

Do you pick up from Lombok International Airport (LOP)?

Yes. Your driver meets you in the arrivals hall with a name board, helps with luggage and takes you directly to your accommodation or harbour.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

We monitor your flight, so your driver adjusts to your actual arrival time.

Can you take us from the airport to the Gili Islands?

We drive you to Teluk Nare or Bangsal harbour in North Lombok, where you can take a boat to the Gili Islands. We can also arrange a private boat charter.

Which car should I choose for a family with luggage?

For up to seven people with luggage, choose a seven-seat car such as the Xpander, Innova or Innova Zenix. For extra comfort, the Toyota Alphard has a premium cabin and captain seats.
